How does Hex compare to Snowflake Notebooks?

Data Exploration in Snowflake

Multiple products

Snowflake notebooks are best for one-off, individual projects for SQL or Python experts who work in Snowflake. Multiple tools are required when these projects need to be deployed at scale (such as Streamlit for apps or third-party BI tools for reporting).

Hex

One Platform for all data workflows

Hex is a platform designed for multiple personas - including data scientists and business stakeholders - to collaborate on data in one environment. It contains all the tools needed for data teams to create analysis, build apps and reports, govern data access and share with others.
